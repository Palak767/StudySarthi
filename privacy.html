Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains, plainly, what data StudySarthi handles and where it goes. Short version: everything currently stays in your own browser — nothing is sent to a server, because there isn't one yet.

What data is stored, and where

The tools below use your browser's localStorage, which only your browser can read, and only stays on the device you're using:

ToolWhat's stored locally
Smart NotesNote titles, subjects, and content
Internship TrackerCompany, role, status, dates, and notes you enter
Goal PlannerGoals and completion history
Coding PracticeLogged problems, platform, difficulty, and dates
SettingsProfile fields and preferences you fill in

What this means in practice

  • No data is transmitted to any server — there's no backend to receive it.
  • Data doesn't sync across devices or browsers.
  • Clearing your browser's site data, cache, or switching devices will erase it, since it isn't backed up anywhere else.
  • No cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts are currently used on this site.

The login/signup page

The current login/signup flow is a static frontend preview — it doesn't send your name, email, or password anywhere, and there's no real account system behind it yet.

When this will change

Once real authentication and a backend (e.g. Firebase or Supabase) are added, some data will move to a real database so it can sync across your devices. This policy will be updated at that point to explain exactly what's collected, how it's secured, and how you can request deletion.

Questions

Reach out via the Contact page with any privacy questions.
