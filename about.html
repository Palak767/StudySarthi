About

Why StudySarthi exists

StudySarthi started from a simple annoyance: keeping track of notes, internship applications, coding practice, and daily goals meant bouncing between five different apps and losing time to context-switching instead of actually studying.

This project is a solo build — an all-in-one student workspace that puts those pieces in one place. It's currently a personal/internship project and a work in progress, not a finished commercial product, and it's built and maintained by one person.

What's real right now

Smart Notes, the Internship Tracker, Goal Planner, Coding Practice log, and Progress Analytics are fully working tools — they save your data locally in your browser using localStorage, so there's no account sync across devices yet. The AI Study Assistant is currently a rule-based preview, clearly labeled as such, while a real AI-powered version is planned.

What's next

  • Real account authentication (Firebase or Supabase), so your data syncs across devices
  • A genuine LLM-powered Study Assistant
  • Continued mobile and accessibility improvements
